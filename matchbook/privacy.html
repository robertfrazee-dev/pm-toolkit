Privacy

Last updated October 14, 2026

Matchbook helps a group pick a place to go. It has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Here is everything it keeps and why.

What you give us

  • Your first name, so people in your deck know who swiped. It is stored with the deck.
  • Your swipes and rankings for each deck you join.
  • Saved places you star are kept only on your device. We never see your list.
  • A random ID saved on your device so the app remembers which swipes are yours. It is not tied to your identity.
  • Your location, only if you tap Near me. It is used to search for places and saved as the center of that deck, rounded to about 100 meters.
  • A push notification token, only if you turn on notifications in the iPhone app. It lets us tell you when it's time to rank or when a winner is picked.

How long we keep it

Decks, names, swipes and rankings are deleted automatically 7 days after a deck is created. Notification tokens are deleted after 90 days without use, or as soon as Apple tells us the app was removed.

Who we share it with

  • People in your deck see your first name, whether you've finished, and your rankings.
  • Google Maps receives your search (category, area or location, and radius) to find places. Place details and photos come from Google. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Apple delivers notifications if you turn them on.
  • Our hosting providers (Vercel and Upstash) store the data above to run the service.
  • Booking sites (OpenTable, Resy, Viator, Uber) only if you tap one of their buttons. You leave Matchbook for their site, which may know you came from us so we can earn a referral fee. We count how many times each kind of button is tapped, with nothing about who tapped it.

We never sell your data or use it for advertising.

Your choices

You can use Matchbook without location by typing an area, and without notifications. To delete a deck early or ask a question, open an issue at github.com/robertfrazee-dev/pm-toolkit/issues.

Children

Matchbook is not directed at children under 13.
