## Q1. Per-competitor breakdown: features, pricing, praise, complaints

### Takeaway
Each tool covers one slice of the job: voice capture (Todoist Ramble), visual day planning (Tiimo, Structured), task breakdown (Goblin Tools), calendar auto-scheduling (Motion, Akiflow, Sunsama), notes plus memory (Mem, Reflect), or general memory with weak reminders (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini). The complaints repeat across tools: price and billing, sync bugs, AI that is "rigid/inaccurate" or "adds work", and reminders that are unreliable or capped.

### Cited Findings

**Todoist (Ramble voice capture, Assist)**
- Ramble launched in January 2026 on iOS, Android, macOS, Windows and web — [Asian Efficiency](https://www.asianefficiency.com/task-management/todoist-ai-setup-guide/)
- Official claim: Ramble understands natural speech in 40 languages and picks up project names, dates, times, priorities and labels — [Todoist: Meet Ramble](https://www.todoist.com/ramble) (vendor page)

## Q3. What happened to Mem.ai and "AI organizes your notes"? Did auto-organization fail, and why?

### Takeaway
Mem did not shut down. It is live as Mem 2.0, released between late 2025 and early 2026 (sources disagree on the date). The first-generation product lost users mainly because of poor execution: lag, sync and data inconsistencies, and weak communication. ChatGPT-era LLMs commoditised its core features. Auto-organisation itself is still described as "often impressive but not always accurate", and it frustrates users who want control.

### Cited Findings
- "Even though the idea was amazing, there was a huge problem with the execution." Early users flocked in at launch — [Daniel Asgharian, Medium](https://medium.com/@danielasgharian/mem-was-going-to-be-the-best-note-taking-app-but-it-did-not-happen-fc67a53edc39)
- After ChatGPT launched, "more applications started doing a lot of the same things as Mem" — [same Medium post](https://medium.com/@danielasgharian/mem-was-going-to-be-the-best-note-taking-app-but-it-did-not-happen-fc67a53edc39)
- One user removed Mem from their stack, citing a "lack of updates and communication" — [Toolify](https://www.toolify.ai/ai-news/boost-your-productivity-discover-why-we-removed-mem-17174)
- Mem 2.0 "overhauled the core engine for speed and stability", addressing "sluggishness and data inconsistencies" — [Saner blog: Mem reviews](https://blog.saner.ai/mem-ai-reviews/) **[competitor]**; the same author is "finally excited" again about 2.0 — [Medium](https://medium.com/@danielasgharian/mem-ai-2-0-why-im-finally-excited-about-this-note-taking-app-again-2f1e0e90cc87)
- Mem moved to collections, where a note can sit in many collections — [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/mem-self-organizing-workspace/id1578757028)
- Auto-organisation is "not always perfectly accurate" — [launched.tools](https://launched.tools/tools/mem); it "occasionally groups things oddly" — [fahimai](https://www.fahimai.com/mem-ai)
- Lingering criticisms: customer support and limited integrations — [aiwiki](https://aiwiki.ai/wiki/mem_ai) / [agentsai.fyi](https://agentsai.fyi/agents/mem-ai)
- Total funding of about $29M (secondary, unverified) — [aiwiki](https://aiwiki.ai/wiki/mem_ai)

### Inferences
- The lesson for Fragments: auto-filing must be fast, visible and easy to correct. It should probably show "filed to X — undo/move" rather than file silently, so users never feel they lost something. Reliability and speed matter more than cleverness. Memory alone is not a moat once ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini have it.

### Gaps
- No reliable source was found on Mem layoffs, user counts, or an official post-mortem.
- Other "AI organizes notes" products were not covered (e.g., Notion AI auto-tagging, Tana supertags, Capacities).

## Q4. Recurring review complaints across the category

### Takeaway
Six complaints recur across the category: (1) price, subscription fatigue and billing traps; (2) sync and data-loss bugs; (3) AI that is rigid, inaccurate or adds work; (4) overwhelming complexity; (5) reminders that are unreliable, capped or cause alarm fatigue; (6) poor mobile apps. Privacy did not surface strongly in the results found.

### Cited Findings
- **Price and billing:** Motion auto-renewal and refund complaints dominate Trustpilot one-star reviews — [HireKai](https://hirekai.ai/blog/motion-app-review). Motion credit overages — [Softcrit](https://softcrit.com/review/motion-review/). Tiimo "aggressive subscription screens" — [mwm.ai](https://mwm.ai/apps/tiimo-ai-planner-to-do/1480220328). Structured lifetime tripled — [HabitBox](https://habitbox.app/blog/structured-app-review).
- **Data loss and sync:** Goblin Tools update "completely wiped out ALL of my to do lists" — [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.goblintools&hl=en_US). Tiimo calendar and Watch sync bugs — [mwm.ai](https://mwm.ai/apps/tiimo-ai-planner-to-do/1480220328). Mem "data inconsistencies" — [Saner blog](https://blog.saner.ai/mem-ai-reviews/).
- **AI quality:** Tiimo's AI planner is "too rigid and inaccurate" — [mwm.ai](https://mwm.ai/apps/tiimo-ai-planner-to-do/1480220328). Motion's AI produces "irrelevant replies or unnecessary tasks" — [G2](https://www.g2.com/products/motionapp/pricing). Mem "groups things oddly" — [fahimai](https://www.fahimai.com/mem-ai). Goblin breakdown goes "deeper than is useful" — [FocusHack](https://www.focushack.io/reviews/goblin-tools-adhd-review/).
- **Complexity:** Akiflow "can be overwhelming" — [Morgen](https://www.morgen.so/blog-posts/sunsama-vs-akiflow).
- **Reminders:** ChatGPT reminders broke when their thread was deleted, plus caps and hibernation — [OpenAI Community](https://community.openai.com/t/chatgpt-tasks-gpt-4o-with-scheduled-tasks-broken/1091150); [Android Authority](https://www.androidauthority.com/chatgpt-scheduled-tasks-hands-on-3682039/). "A wall of daily notifications trains you to ignore all of them" — [Braindump blog](https://braindumpnotes.app/blog/best-adhd-reminder-apps) **[vendor-authored]**.
- **Mobile:** Motion is rated 2.7/5 on Google Play — [Max Productive](https://max-productive.ai/ai-tools/motion-ai/).
- **Crowded "AI second brain for ADHD" space:** Saner, Recordo, 2ndBrainAI, Braindump, Pocket Brain, Jot and Ordisio all pitch voice-first ADHD capture — [2ndbrainai.app](https://2ndbrainai.app/); [Recordo](https://recordo.app/en/); [Braindump](https://braindumpnotes.app/blog/best-adhd-reminder-apps). Usage caps are common (e.g., Braindump free tier allows 3 web transcriptions a day; Ordisio caps 400 dumps a month) — [Braindump](https://braindumpnotes.app/blog/best-adhd-reminder-apps).

### Inferences
- **Gap analysis for Fragments:**
  1. **Memory plus structure plus reminders together.** LLM assistants remember but can't reliably remind or file. Task apps file and remind but don't remember.
  2. **Trustworthy auto-filing.** Show every filing decision with one-tap correction, and never lose data. Mem and Goblin's trust failures show what happens otherwise.
  3. **Wrist-first capture.** Apple Watch and Siri capture is under-served and buggy (Tiimo), and Apple's own contextual Siri is late.
  4. **Calm reminders, not walls of alerts.** Address alarm fatigue with fewer, context-timed nudges.
  5. **"Does the work".** Motion's AI Employees are judged costly and noisy, so there is an opening for scoped, useful outputs such as plans and drafts tied to captured context.
  6. **Pricing.** Avoid credit overages and aggressive paywalls. A free capture tier plus a fair Pro price, or a lifetime option, would answer the subscription fatigue.
  7. **Competition warning.** Many small voice-first "ADHD second brain" apps launched in 2025–26, so differentiation has to rest on memory quality and reliability, not on the pitch.

### Gaps
- Privacy complaints did not surface in these searches. Expect them to matter for always-listening or memory products, but there is no evidence here.
- No quantitative cross-app complaint dataset was found, apart from Soothfy's Tiimo sample (100 reviews).
- No real Reddit threads were retrievable.
